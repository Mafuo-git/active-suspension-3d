Tap a coloured point in 3D or in the list, then drag the arrows. Values in mm for the left side (x forward, y left, z up, origin on the ground at the front axle). The right side is mirrored.

Pickup points (hard points) are where the links attach to the chassis and to the upright. Moving them changes how the wheel moves: watch camber gain, bump steer, roll centre and motion ratio in the Analysis tab.
